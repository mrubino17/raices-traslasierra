# Project brief — Raíces Traslasierra

## Overview
A real estate website prototype focused on the Traslasierra region of Córdoba, Argentina.

## Audience
- Primary: people looking to buy or sell property.
- Secondary: investors and people seeking a second home.

## Positioning
A locally focused real estate service that helps people find a place in Traslasierra, not simply browse listings.

## Brand
- Name: Raíces Traslasierra
- Working tagline: Tu lugar en Traslasierra.
- Personality: approachable, professional and transparent.
- Language: Spanish for the first release.

## Visual direction
Contemporary natural, with editorial and architectural influences. Use authentic regional landscapes and properties, natural light, restrained saturation and honest representation.

## Initial sitemap
1. Home
2. Property catalogue
3. Property detail
4. About
5. Contact / owner enquiry

## Homepage content direction
- Regional hero with a clear property-search entry point.
- Featured properties.
- Editorial introduction to the region.
- Credible reasons to work with the business.
- Separate calls to action for buyers and owners.
- Contact details and legal information.

## Future WordPress model
Potential custom post type: Properties.
Potential taxonomies: Location, Operation, Property type.
Potential fields: price, area, rooms, gallery and location.

The exact business claims, contact details, property inventory and service coverage must be confirmed before publication.
